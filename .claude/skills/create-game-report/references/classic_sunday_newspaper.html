ROCKETS SURVIVE MAGIC IN OVERTIME THRILLER

Şengün forces OT at the buzzer; Durant and Thompson seal the victory in a 117-113 classic.

In a contest that will be remembered long after the season's end, the Houston Rockets outlasted a tenacious Orlando Magic squad at the Toyota Center on Sunday night. It was a game defined by sheer willpower, shifting momentums, and a breathtaking final minute of regulation that left the crowd in absolute awe.

Trailing by three with less than half a minute remaining, the Rockets turned to the veteran poise of Kevin Durant, who calmly sank a twenty-six-foot jumper to knot the score. The Magic answered immediately, as Anthony Black drove the lane for a resounding dunk with a single second left on the clock.

But it was Alperen Şengün who delivered the final word in regulation. Catching an inbound pass with no margin for error, Şengün lofted a five-foot hook shot that found the bottom of the net as the buzzer sounded, sending the arena into a frenzy and the game into an extra period.

In overtime, Houston's experience shone through. Durant struck again with a critical mid-range jumper, and Amen Thompson showed ice in his veins by sinking two free throws in the closing moments to secure the 117-113 victory. The Magic, led by Franz Wagner and Desmond Bane, fought valiantly, but fell just short in the trenches.
